# Privacy Policy

We take your privacy seriously. This privacy policy applies to the Trajectory IR documentation and software.

## Data Collection
As an open-source tool, Trajectory IR runs entirely on your own infrastructure. We do not collect telemetry, usage metrics, or any personally identifiable information from your instances by default.

## Website Visitors
When you visit this documentation website, we may collect standard web analytics to help us improve the documentation structure and content.

## Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us on GitHub.
